\section{System Configuration}
\label{app:system}

\paragraph{Models.}
All generation, tool-selection and critic roles used
\texttt{openai/gpt-oss-20b} served via Groq (set through environment overrides
of the \textsc{tool}, \textsc{synth} and \textsc{critic} model roles). The
judge-side experiment (\S\ref{sec:results}) additionally used
\texttt{llama-3.1-8b-instant}. Groq inference is not seed-deterministic at
temperature $>0$; we therefore report over repeated runs rather than fixed
seeds.

\paragraph{Retrieval.}
Dense embeddings use \texttt{sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2}
(384-dimensional); reranking uses the cross-encoder
\texttt{cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2}. The hybrid candidate pool is
$K_{\text{cand}}=15$, fused by reciprocal-rank fusion with constant $k=60$
(i.e.\ $1/(60+\mathrm{rank})$), then reranked and truncated to the top
$\textsc{top-}k=3$ parent passages returned to the generator. Documents are
chunked into parents of $\approx 800$ words and children of $\approx 200$ words
with a 40-word child overlap; children are indexed and matched, parents are
returned. The corpus is the IDMC Global Report on Internal Displacement 2024,
yielding 77 parent and 357 child chunks.

\paragraph{Reasoning.}
Synthesis uses a few-shot chain-of-thought format with self-consistency at
$k=3$ and temperature $0.8$; tool selection uses temperature $0.0$. The final
answer is the medoid of the $k$ conclusions under embedding similarity. The
agent loop is bounded at $\textsc{max-steps}=6$.

\paragraph{Budget and cost.}
A per-run token budget is enforced by a \texttt{TokenBudget} guard with a hard
cap of \$2.00 and a warning threshold of \$0.50, priced at
\$0.59/\$0.79 per million input/output tokens. Measured cost of the judge-side
experiment (S2) was approximately \$0.12 for 539 judge scores. Total inference
cost across all experiments: \ph{S6: sum once S3/S4 have run}.
