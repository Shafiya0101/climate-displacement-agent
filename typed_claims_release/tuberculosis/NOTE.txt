probe_tb.json
  38 items built from who_prev_notif_source.csv.
  Median near delta 0.57, median far delta 19.9. 25 STOCK golds, 13 FLOW.

  Types are schema-given: the WHO 'prev' column is a prevalence (STOCK) and
  'notified' counts cases reported during the year (FLOW). No cue words and
  no annotator are involved.

  Absolute prevalent cases = prev / 100000 * pop.

  Near distractors: same country, same year, opposite temporal operator.
  Far distractors: a different country (one survey per country), so they
  differ in geography as well as temporal operator. The NEAR condition is
  the clean one.
