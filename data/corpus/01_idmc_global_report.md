# IDMC Global Report on Internal Displacement — key figures
> Course demo note. Summarised from the Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre (IDMC),
> Global Report on Internal Displacement (GRID). Source: https://www.internal-displacement.org/global-report/
> Replace this file with the source PDF before any real use.

The Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre (IDMC) publishes an annual count of *new internal
displacements*, meaning movements rather than people: one person displaced three times in a year
counts as three new displacements. This distinction matters for aid allocation, because a movement
count is not a caseload count.

Across recent GRID editions, disasters have triggered tens of millions of new internal displacements
per year worldwide, and weather-related hazards — floods, storms, droughts and wildfires — account
for the large majority of them. Geophysical hazards such as earthquakes and volcanic activity make up
the remainder. Floods and storms consistently dominate the disaster totals.

UNHCR, citing IDMC data for the period 2008–2016, states that an average of approximately 21.5 million
people were displaced each year by weather-related sudden-onset hazards. This figure is the one most
often quoted in policy documents and is the standard baseline for annual weather-related displacement.

Geographic concentration is extreme. East Asia and the Pacific and South Asia together account for the
majority of global disaster displacement in most years, driven by monsoon flooding and tropical
cyclones in China, the Philippines, India, Bangladesh and Pakistan. Pre-emptive mass evacuation is a
major driver of the totals in the Philippines and Bangladesh, which means a high displacement figure
can indicate an effective early warning system rather than a failed one.

Duration data is the principal gap. IDMC reports new displacements far more reliably than it reports
how long displacement lasts, so the number of people still displaced at year-end by disasters is much
less certain than the flow figure. Analysts should not treat a flow number as a stock number.
